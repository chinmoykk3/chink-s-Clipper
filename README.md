# ⚡ Chink's Clipper

Transform long-form YouTube videos into high-retention, viral **9:16 Shorts, Reels, and TikToks** — 100% free with NVIDIA RTX 4060 hardware acceleration.

---

## 🌟 Key Features
- **🏠 Home Feed & Gallery:** Clean, Instagram/TikTok-style mobile showcase of all previously rendered shorts with stats, playback, and download buttons.
- **🎬 AI Director (Gemini 3.8 Flash):** Strictly identifies the **3-second hook**, rising context, and crisp punchline resolution.
- **📌 Scroll-Stopping Hook Overlays:** Automatically generates dynamic 3–5 word headlines displayed across the top during the crucial first 3.5 seconds.
- **💥 Kinetic Typography:** Word-grouped captions with high-contrast outlines and colored highlights on dynamic power words.
- **🎥 Studio Framing Options:**
  - **Dynamic Blurred Backdrop:** Modern studio look with full 16:9 frame centered over an aesthetically blurred, scaled backdrop.
  - **Smart Crop:** Direct 9:16 center framing.
- **⚡ Hardware Acceleration (NVENC):** Fast rendering on your local RTX 4060 GPU.
- **💸 100% Free:** Completely free local pipeline and Google AI Studio free tier.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start

### 1. Launch the App
Double-click `run.bat` or run in PowerShell:
```powershell
cd "F:\chink's Clipper"
.\.venv\Scripts\streamlit run app.py
```

### 2. Enter Free Gemini API Key
- Get your free key at: [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey) (No credit card required).
- Paste it into the left sidebar in the web interface.

### 3. Paste Any YouTube Link & Click Analyze!
- Choose your preferred layout mode.
- Preview the AI-detected viral hooks and retention scores.
- Click **"Render 9:16 Short"** to generate mobile-ready MP4 clips that automatically save to your **Home Feed**!
